# Returns & Refunds Policy

Effective for all orders placed through our online store.

## Return window
Customers may return any item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided the item is unused and in its original packaging. Items returned
after 30 days but within 60 days are eligible for store credit only.

## Non-returnable items
Custom-configured laptops (Laptop Pro with non-standard specs) and opened
software licenses are final sale and cannot be returned.

## Refund processing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days
of us receiving the returned item. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless
the return is due to a defect or our error.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 48 hours of
delivery with photos of the damage. We cover return shipping in these cases
and will send a replacement or refund immediately upon confirmation, without
waiting for the returned item to arrive.

## International orders
International returns follow the same 30-day window, but the customer is
responsible for return shipping costs and any customs fees. Refunds exclude
original international shipping charges.

## Warranty claims
Laptop Pro and Tablet A carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering
hardware defects. Phone X carries a 2-year warranty. Warranty claims are
handled separately from returns — contact support to start a warranty claim
rather than a standard return.
