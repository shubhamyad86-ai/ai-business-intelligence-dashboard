# Shipping Policy

## Domestic shipping (Germany, France, Spain)
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and costs €4.99, free on orders
over €75. Express shipping (1-2 business days) costs €14.99 regardless of
order size.

## Order processing time
Orders placed before 2 PM CET ship the same business day. Orders placed
after 2 PM, or on weekends and holidays, ship the next business day.

## International shipping
We currently ship to all EU countries. International standard shipping
takes 7-14 business days and costs €19.99. Customers are responsible for
any import duties or taxes charged by their local customs authority.

## Order tracking
A tracking link is emailed automatically once an order ships. Tracking
updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the email is sent.

## Lost or delayed packages
If a package hasn't arrived within 3 business days of the estimated
delivery date, contact support and we will investigate with the carrier.
Packages confirmed lost by the carrier are replaced at no cost or refunded,
customer's choice.

## Address changes
Shipping addresses can be changed only if the order hasn't shipped yet.
Once an order ships, we're unable to redirect it — contact the carrier
directly using the tracking link.
